TITLE
PAIR: Verifiable quantitative knowledge and model comparison from incomplete experimental references

ABSTRACT
Knowledge-based systems need quantitative assertions whose numerical meaning remains valid when experimental references are incomplete. We introduce PAIR (Projection-Aligned Identifiable Relations), connecting verifiable knowledge compilation with model comparison through a shared reference geometry. PAIR-KG retains complete linear comparisons, binds them to a registered completion and local witness, and verifies reference estimability and declared shared-source cancellation. Its properties include aggregation closure, source-refinement monotonicity, universal completion conditions and explicit operator-application costs. PAIR-Eval uses the dual quotient space to separate resolved prediction error from training-unresolved offsets and characterize model-order invariance under truth-only completion shifts, including three-way preferences on bounded domains. K562 and ENCODE retain 2,635 (26.50%) and 56,395 (5.79%) compound-only dimensions beyond individually estimable effects. Replogle metadata establish shared-control cancellation for 100,000 registered four-cell designs. For seven fixed CTRPv2 predictors on registered model-specific supports, full-to-resolved scoring reverses median fractions of 50.0% of model-pair orders under normalized R² and 35.7% under normalized Pearson correlation, with five persistent pair–metric reversals. GDSC2 exhibits 30.0% median fold-level reversals; persistence checks distinguish target sensitivity from recurring model preferences. PAIR preserves the comparative information needed to verify quantitative knowledge and interpret predictive decisions.
